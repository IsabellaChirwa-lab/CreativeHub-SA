What is Creative Hub ZA?

Musicians, photographers, tattoo artists, dancers, painters, filmmakers, designers and 3D artists each get a public profile with their links and pictures of their work.

Labels, radio stations, brands and everyday clients can browse that wall, message creatives directly, post a brief for paid work and request a booking.

We are onboarding the first creatives now, so the platform is full of talent the day it opens.

Set up in about two minutes

  1. 1Create your account

    Choose who you are joining as, then add your name, email and city.

  2. 2Show your work

    Add your Spotify, YouTube or Instagram links and up to six pictures.

  3. 3Take your place on the wall

    Upload a photo. Visitors who tap your tile land on your profile.
